## One orchestration platform

Scrums.com is the enterprise AI platform for software engineering. SEOP, the Enterprise AI Software Engineering Orchestration Platform, is the architecture of that platform: one control plane for AI agents, models, engineers and infrastructure. It gives engineering and product leaders one place to deploy, measure, govern, and scale software delivery.

The platform coordinates three commercial surfaces:

| Surface | What you deploy |
|---|---|
| [AI Catalog](https://www.scrums.com/catalog) | Agents, models, MCP servers, skills, APIs, and infrastructure |
| [AI Development](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development) | Defined scopes and managed delivery |
| [Hire](https://www.scrums.com/hire) | Engineers and teams |

Platform capabilities such as the [AI Agent Gateway](/platform/ai-gateway), [DORA metrics](/platform/dora-metrics), [developer productivity](/platform/developer-productivity), [capacity planning](/platform/engineering-capacity-planning), the [agent harness](/platform/agent-harness), and the [MCP Gateway](/platform/mcp-gateway) operate across those deployments.

## The operating model

1. **Orchestrate.** Select what you need and deploy it into a workspace.
2. **Measure.** Connect delivery tools and observe outcomes through shared telemetry.
3. **Govern.** Apply workspace roles, policies, approvals, and audit controls.
4. **Scale.** Add or change capacity as your roadmap and constraints change.

## Platform and catalog are different

The platform is the control plane. The catalog contains deployable records. A platform subscription gives you the workspace, governance, intelligence, and support needed to operate catalog deployments.

See [Plans and subscription](/platform/plans) for plan behavior and [Catalog overview](/catalog/overview) for catalog classes.
