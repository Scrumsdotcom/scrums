## One subscription, not a product list

**Platform is a single bundled subscription.** You subscribe to Platform. You then deploy items from the four catalog registers — Agents, Delivery, Talent and Infra — against that subscription.

Platform is the control plane. Unlike the AI Catalog, AI Development, and Hire surfaces, it holds no deployable catalog records. It is the layer those deployments run on. See [AI Catalog Overview](/catalog/overview).

This is why there is no separate price for a capability. Capabilities such as DORA metrics or the AI agent harness are part of Platform, not products beside it.

## What the tiers change

Every tier gives you the same platform. The tiers differ on **governance, accountability and where the platform runs** — not on which primitives you may use.

| Tier | Positioning |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Scope the platform and catalog before you commit. |
| **Access** | Deploy your first catalog primitive on a card. |
| **Business** | Full governance and intelligence for multi-team delivery. |
| **Enterprise** | Your environment, your controls, named accountability. |

The progression is deliberate:

- **Free → Access** unlocks deployment. You move from looking at the catalog to running something from it.
- **Access → Business** unlocks governance and delivery intelligence across several teams.
- **Business → Enterprise** unlocks your own environment, your own controls, and named accountability with an SLA.

<Note>
Current prices, tier limits and SLA terms live on the [pricing page](https://www.scrums.com/pricing). They are deliberately not duplicated here — a number in two places drifts.
</Note>

## What does not change with tier

- **The catalog.** Every tier sees all four registers.
- **Identity.** Deployments carry a permanent `DEP-` code at every tier. See [Deployments](/products/deployments).
- **Model choice.** Platform is model-agnostic at every tier.

## Usage

Deployments are billed against your subscription, not bundled into it. A subscription grants access to the platform and its governance; each deployment is its own commercial line.

See [Billing & Usage](/administration/billing) for invoicing terms and reconciliation.
