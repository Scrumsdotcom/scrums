Quality assurance is delivered as scoped workstreams on the Scrums.com platform: test automation frameworks, AI-generated regression suites, CI/CD quality gates, and performance and security testing. Every test run, coverage metric and defect trend is visible in one dashboard.

This service runs on a [Delivery Plan](/ai-development/delivery-plans). One plan gives you one active workstream: a managed team, one delivery owner and one prioritised backlog that also holds your other development work. You queue requests or [scopes](/ai-development/scopes), and the team delivers them one at a time, in your order. First code reaches production within 48 hours of access. The first agreed deliverable ships within 14 days, or the first month is free. For parallel workstreams, use [Enterprise delivery](/ai-development/enterprise-delivery). Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://www.scrums.com/pricing).
