## Overview

An Outcome-Driven Sprint is a time-boxed execution model for a defined scope. It works best when you can describe the result, required inputs, and acceptance criteria before delivery begins.

Examples include discovery, architecture, audits, prototypes, remediation, and technical-debt reduction.

## Before the sprint

Confirm:

- The outcome and acceptance criteria
- Included and excluded work
- Required repositories, environments, and stakeholders
- Known dependencies and risks
- Deliverable format and review date
- What happens when assumptions change

## During and after delivery

Scrums.com tracks the sprint as a deployment. At the final review, you accept the deliverables, request changes within the agreed scope, or define a follow-on deployment.

An Outcome-Driven Sprint is one scope model. The broader [AI Development scopes](/ai-development/scopes) register can contain other delivery shapes.
