UI/UX design is delivered as scoped workstreams on the Scrums.com platform: user research, interactive prototypes, design systems and accessibility, validated with real users and handed to engineering ready to build. AI agents check accessibility and component compliance on every iteration.

This service runs on a [Delivery Plan](/ai-development/overview#the-delivery-plan). One plan gives you one active workstream: a managed team, one delivery owner and one prioritised backlog that also holds your other development work. You queue requests or [scopes](/ai-development/scopes), and the team delivers them one at a time, in your order. First code reaches production within 48 hours of access. The first agreed deliverable ships within 14 days, or the first month is free. For parallel workstreams, use [enterprise delivery](/ai-development/overview#enterprise-delivery). The current price is on the [AI Development page](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development#pricing) and on each service page.
