## Delivery through defined scopes

AI Development contains engineering work that Scrums.com owns and delivers. You define the desired outcome, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Scrums.com provides the delivery structure, people, agents, and reporting.

Use AI Development when you need an outcome rather than an individual engineer.

## Common delivery areas

- AI product and agent engineering
- Software product development
- Application modernization
- Web and mobile development
- Data engineering and analytics
- DevOps and platform engineering
- Software testing and QA
- Maintenance and support
- Product and experience design

## Choose an engagement

- Use a [scope](/ai-development/scopes) for a bounded, clearly accepted outcome.
- Use [managed delivery](/ai-development/managed-delivery) for ongoing operational ownership.
- Use [Hire](/hire/overview) when you want to manage an engineer or team directly.
