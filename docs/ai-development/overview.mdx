## How it works

AI Development services are engineering work that Scrums.com delivers and you govern on the Scrums.com Platform. All services run on the same commercial model:

1. **[The Delivery Plan](#the-delivery-plan).** One monthly plan gives you one active workstream: a managed team, one delivery owner and one prioritised backlog.
2. **[Scopes](/ai-development/scopes).** A scope is a defined unit of work with an outcome and acceptance criteria. You queue published scopes or your own requests in the plan backlog.
3. **[Enterprise delivery](#enterprise-delivery).** For several workstreams in parallel, custom terms or a security review, Scrums.com quotes an enterprise agreement.

Use AI Development when you need an outcome. Use [Hire](/hire/overview) when you want to manage an engineer or a team yourself.

## The Delivery Plan

A Delivery Plan is a monthly subscription for development capacity. It is not a fixed price for a project. Your scope sets the number of months.

Delivery Plan · Core includes:

- One active workstream: a managed team that works on your highest-priority request until it is live, then starts the next.
- Unlimited requests in your backlog. You set the order.
- One delivery owner who runs the team and confirms each scope with you.
- AI agents for QA and code review in the delivery loop.
- Delivery tracking on the Scrums.com Platform: milestones, releases and DORA metrics.
- Ownership of all code, designs and deliverables from day one.

One plan covers every service on this page. Mobile, web, backend, AI, data, QA and DevOps work can share one backlog.

### How work moves through the plan

1. You add a request or a [scope](/ai-development/scopes) to the backlog.
2. Your delivery owner confirms the outcome and the acceptance criteria with you.
3. The team delivers the top item. You review and approve it.
4. The next item starts when the current item ships.

You can reorder any item that has not started. More requests mean more months, not faster delivery.

### The first 14 days

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Onboarding starts at checkout. You confirm the first scope and give access to repositories, cloud accounts and CI. |
| Within 48 hours | First change merged and deployed through your pipeline. |
| Within 14 days | The first agreed deliverable ships. |
| If it does not ship | You do not pay for the first month. |

The clocks start when scope and access are confirmed.

### Billing

The plan is billed monthly. Prices exclude VAT or sales tax. The current price is on the [AI Development page](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development#pricing) and on each service page on www.scrums.com.

## Enterprise delivery

Use enterprise delivery when one workstream is not enough or when your organization needs custom terms. Common reasons:

- Several workstreams in parallel, for example a product build and a modernization programme at the same time.
- Service levels for production systems across several teams.
- A security, compliance or procurement review before work starts.
- One contract and one invoice across AI Development services, Hire and the Scrums.com Platform.

Each workstream runs on the Delivery Plan model, with its own delivery owner and backlog. All workstreams report on the Scrums.com Platform. Enterprise pricing is quoted. [Talk to sales →](https://www.scrums.com/contact-us?intent=sales)
