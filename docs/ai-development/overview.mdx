## How it works

AI Development services are engineering work that Scrums.com delivers and you govern on the Scrums.com Platform. All services run on the same commercial model:

1. **[Delivery Plan](/ai-development/delivery-plans).** One monthly plan gives you one active workstream: a managed team, one delivery owner and one prioritised backlog.
2. **[Scopes](/ai-development/scopes).** A scope is a defined unit of work with an outcome and acceptance criteria. You queue published scopes or your own requests in the plan backlog.
3. **[Enterprise delivery](/ai-development/enterprise-delivery).** For several workstreams in parallel, custom terms or a security review, Scrums.com quotes an enterprise agreement.

The path for a new plan:

1. Start a Delivery Plan. Onboarding starts at checkout.
2. Confirm the first scope with your delivery owner. Give access to repositories, cloud accounts and CI.
3. First code reaches production within 48 hours of access.
4. The first agreed deliverable ships within 14 days, or the first month is free.
5. The team delivers the backlog in your order. You track every release on the Scrums.com Platform.

Use AI Development when you need an outcome. Use [Hire](/hire/overview) when you want to manage an engineer or a team yourself.
