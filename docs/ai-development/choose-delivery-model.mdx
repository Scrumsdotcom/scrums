## Start with ownership

Choose a delivery model based on the unit you need and who should own day-to-day delivery.

| Need | Best starting point | Day-to-day ownership |
|---|---|---|
| A bounded engineering outcome | [AI Development scope](/ai-development/scopes) | Scrums.com |
| Ongoing ownership of a delivery function | [Managed delivery](/ai-development/managed-delivery) | Scrums.com |
| A specialist inside your existing team | [Engineer](/hire/engineers) | You |
| A complete, ongoing delivery unit | [Dedicated team](/hire/teams) | Defined during scoping |

## Questions to answer

1. Can you define the output and acceptance criteria?
2. Do you want to manage the work or the outcome?
3. Is the need bounded or ongoing?
4. Do you need one skill or a complete delivery unit?
5. Which systems, environments, and approvals are required?

You can combine models across separate service lines. Keep ownership and acceptance criteria explicit for each deployment.
