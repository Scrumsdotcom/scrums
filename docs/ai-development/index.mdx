A Delivery Plan is a subscription to delivery capacity. You queue requests; Scrums.com scopes them, builds them, and ships them to your repositories. Every request is tracked from scope to live in the platform, under **Delivery Plans** in your workspace.

Use a Delivery Plan when you want an outcome delivered. Use [Hire](/hire/overview) when you want an engineer you manage directly. If you are not sure which fits, see [Choosing a delivery model](/platform/comparing-delivery-models).

The Software Factory is how Scrums.com builds — senior engineers and AI agents working one prioritised backlog together. A Delivery Plan is how you access it. These docs cover the plan.

## The model

A plan buys **one active workstream**: one managed team working your highest-priority request until it is live, then starting the next. Your backlog is unlimited — the workstream is the constraint. More requests mean more months, not faster delivery.

One plan covers every discipline. Web, mobile, backend, AI, data, QA, DevOps and design share the same backlog and the same team, so work does not need to be split across separate engagements.

## Plan tiers

| Tier | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **Core** | One active workstream. Your highest-priority request is delivered to live, then the next one starts. |
| **Factory** | Multiple workstreams running concurrently against the same backlog, under one delivery owner. Several pieces of work ship at the same time instead of queueing behind each other — user management and OAuth sign-in delivered in parallel rather than one after the other. |

See [What's included](/ai-development/whats-included) for coverage, guarantees and commercial terms.

## From request to live

Every item moves through five states on the delivery board:

| State | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| **Scope** | The request is being defined and estimated. Still yours to edit. |
| **Build** | Engineering is underway. The request locks. |
| **Verify** | QA and review, including AI code review. |
| **Deploy** | Release to your environments. |
| **Deployed** | Live in production and recorded in the deployed log. |

[Tracking delivery](/ai-development/tracking-delivery) covers the board, epics, decisions and risks.

## Scopes

A **scope** is a pre-defined piece of delivery work with a known shape and finish state — a new feature, an old framework upgrade, a new platform, or ongoing operations under a service level. Browse them at [scrums.com/ai-development/scopes](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development/scopes).

Add any scope to your plan. Your delivery team runs it from your prioritised backlog like any other request, **at no additional cost** — scopes are included on every tier. They are a shortcut to a well-defined brief, not a separate purchase.

Scopes are a starting point, not a limit. Work that does not match one is scoped from scratch against the same plan.
