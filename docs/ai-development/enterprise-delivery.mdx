## When to use Enterprise delivery

Use Enterprise delivery when one workstream is not enough or when your organization needs custom terms. Common reasons:

- Several workstreams in parallel, for example a product build and a modernization programme at the same time.
- Service levels for production systems across several teams.
- A security, compliance or procurement review before work starts.
- One contract and one invoice across AI Development services, Hire and the Scrums.com Platform.

## How it works

1. Talk to the Scrums.com enterprise team about your outcomes, systems and constraints.
2. Scrums.com proposes the number of workstreams, the team shape and the governance model.
3. You agree the terms. Each workstream runs on the [Delivery Plan](/ai-development/delivery-plans) model, with its own delivery owner and backlog.
4. All workstreams report on the Scrums.com Platform, with portfolio-level reporting across them.

Enterprise pricing is quoted. It is not on the pricing page.
