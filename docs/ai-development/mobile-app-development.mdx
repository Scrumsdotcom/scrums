iOS, Android and cross-platform apps are delivered as scoped workstreams on the Scrums.com platform: fixed milestones, SLA-backed, shipped to both stores and maintained after launch. You pick the scopes; Scrums.com owns the outcome, with AI agents reviewing, testing and watching every release.

Scrums.com provisions this service as scoped workstreams on the platform. You pick the scopes, Scrums.com owns the outcome milestone by milestone, and every sprint is visible in one dashboard. Each workstream runs on a delivery plan; current plans and prices live on the [pricing page](https://www.scrums.com/pricing). To start, select a scope on the www page or in [Scopes](/ai-development/scopes), complete checkout, and assign the resulting deployment to a workspace. Use [Choose a delivery model](/ai-development/choose-delivery-model) if you are not sure this is the right shape.
