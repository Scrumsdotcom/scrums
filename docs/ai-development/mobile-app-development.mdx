iOS, Android and cross-platform apps are delivered as scoped workstreams on the Scrums.com platform: fixed milestones, SLA-backed, shipped to both stores and maintained after launch. You pick the scopes; Scrums.com owns the outcome, with AI agents reviewing, testing and watching every release.

This service runs on a [Delivery Plan](/ai-development). One plan gives you one active workstream: a managed team, one delivery owner and one prioritised backlog that also holds your other development work. You queue requests or [scopes](/ai-development/scopes), and the team delivers them one at a time, in your order. Your team is connected to your repositories and writing code within 48 hours of access. The first agreed deliverable ships within 14 days, or the first month is free. For parallel workstreams, use the [Factory tier](/ai-development#plan-tiers). The current price is on the [AI Development page](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development#pricing) and on each service page.
