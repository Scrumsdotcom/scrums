## Hire engineering capacity

Hire is the Scrums.com surface for engineers and teams. Use it when you want ongoing capacity and direct participation in your engineering workflow.

| Shape | What you receive |
|---|---|
| [Engineers](/hire/engineers) | One or more specialists embedded in your team |
| [Dedicated teams](/hire/teams) | A complete delivery unit assembled for your roadmap |

Scrums.com supports matching, contracting, onboarding, replacement, and platform reporting. Confirm the management and accountability model during scoping.

## How matching works

Matching considers your required role, seniority, technology, domain experience, location or timezone, availability, and working model. Human review remains part of selection and confirmation.

Current availability and onboarding terms live on [Hire](https://www.scrums.com/hire).
