## When to use a dedicated team

A dedicated team gives you a stable, cross-functional delivery unit for an ongoing roadmap. Use this model when the constraint is a whole team rather than one specialist or one bounded scope.

## Define the team

Agree:

- Required roles and seniority
- Product and technical ownership
- Day-to-day management responsibilities
- Backlog and prioritization process
- Quality, security, and release controls
- Reporting and escalation cadence
- Scaling and replacement procedures

<Note>
Do not infer management ownership from the team label alone. Record whether you, Scrums.com, or both parties manage day-to-day delivery in the service-line agreement.
</Note>

## Onboard the team

Provide product context, architecture, repositories, environments, working agreements, and initial priorities. Start with a small, accepted delivery milestone before expanding access or scope.
