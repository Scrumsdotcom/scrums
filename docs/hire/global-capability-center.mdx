A global capability center (GCC) is a high-performance engineering centre that you own and that Scrums.com operates for you in Africa. Your team, your IP. Scrums.com deploys vetted engineering teams in 21 days and scales them with full engineering visibility, so you get an offshore development centre without building the local entity, hiring pipeline and operations yourself.

The centre is provisioned on the platform as [dedicated teams](/hire/teams), operated across five African regions, with the first team operational in 21 days. You run the capability center from one dashboard: the same [DORA metrics](/platform/dora-metrics), [capacity planning](/platform/engineering-capacity-planning) and governance that apply to every deployment. To start, request a scoping call on the www page and define the roles, ownership and controls described in [Dedicated teams](/hire/teams).
