## Support channels

### In-app chat

The fastest way to get help for platform-related questions. Available via the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of your workspace. Use this for quick questions and platform navigation help.

### Email support

[support@scrums.com](mailto:support@scrums.com) — monitored during business hours (GMT). Response times are set out in the [Support SLA](#support-sla) below.

### Your Enablement Partner

For delivery-related questions, escalations, team performance concerns, and commercial queries, contact your dedicated Enablement Partner directly via email or the shared ClickUp channel for your engagement.

Your Enablement Partner is your primary contact at Scrums.com for anything related to your active delivery teams.

### Emergency support (Enterprise)

Enterprise clients have access to 24/7 premium support with a 4-hour SLA for critical production incidents. Contact details and escalation procedures are provided during Enterprise onboarding.

## Support SLA

This section sets the response times Scrums.com commits to when you log a support issue. The target depends on three things: your plan, the priority of the issue, and whether you log it during or outside operating hours.

<Note>
This section covers **support response times only**. A response means a person has acknowledged the issue and is working on it. It is not a commitment to a fix within that time. Delivery SLAs for Managed Services are set per pod and are not covered here. Your agreement and the [pricing page](https://www.scrums.com/pricing) govern if they differ from this page.
</Note>

### At a glance

| | |

| **Operating hours** | 08:00–17:30 |
| **Time zone** | Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) |
| **Operating days** | Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays |
| **Plans covered** | Free to Enterprise |

### Cover by plan

| Plan | Support | Cover | Response SLA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Community and documentation support | Self-service | No response-time commitment |
| **Access** | Email and ticket | Operating hours | 24 hours |
| **Business** | Priority | Operating hours | 8 hours |
| **Enterprise** | 24/7 with a named escalation contact | Every hour of every day | 4 hours |

Paid plans share the priority targets below. No response is slower than the plan's figure in this table. Where the two differ, the faster one applies.

### Operating hours

The support desk is staffed from **08:00 to 17:30 GMT**, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

Any time outside that window, including weekends and public holidays, is outside operating hours.

Enterprise is the exception. Its cover runs every hour of every day, so operating hours do not limit it.

### Priority levels

Every issue gets one of four priorities when it is logged. Scrums.com confirms or adjusts the priority in its first response, and explains any change.

| Priority | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 · Critical** | The service is down or unusable for all users, or data is at risk. No workaround exists. | Production outage; security breach |
| **P2 · High** | A core function is failing or badly degraded for many users. Any workaround is impractical. | Users cannot log in; payments failing |
| **P3 · Medium** | A function is impaired for some users, but a reasonable workaround exists. | A report exports with errors; a search filter misses some results, but the records can still be found by browsing |
| **P4 · Low** | A minor defect, cosmetic issue or general question with little effect on day-to-day use. | Layout glitch; how-to query |

### Response time targets

The time you log the issue sets the target that applies.

| Priority | Logged during operating hours | Logged outside operating hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** | 1 hour | 15 hours |
| **P2** | 2 hours | 16 hours |
| **P3** | 4 hours | 18 hours |
| **P4** | 8 hours | 24 hours |

Outside-hours targets do not count weekends or public holidays. [How response time is measured](#how-response-time-is-measured) explains how the clock treats them.

Each plan caps these targets at its own figure. Free has no response targets.

| Plan | Cap | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Access** | 24 hours | Nothing. Every target already falls within 24 hours. |
| **Business** | 8 operating hours | An issue logged outside operating hours is answered within 8 operating hours of the desk next opening, if that is sooner than the target above. A P4 logged at 18:00 is due by 16:00 the next working day, not 18:00. |
| **Enterprise** | 4 hours, 24/7 | The during-hours column applies at all times, and a P4 is answered within 4 hours, not 8. |

### How response time is measured

The clock starts when you log the issue by email or ticket with enough detail to act on. It stops when a person at Scrums.com acknowledges the issue, confirms its priority and states the next step. An automated receipt does not count as a response.

**Logged during operating hours.** The target runs in operating hours. If the day ends before the target is reached, the clock pauses at 17:30 and resumes at 08:00 on the next working day. A P4 logged at 16:00 is due by 14:30 the next working day.

**Logged outside operating hours.** The target runs in elapsed hours from the time of logging. A P1 logged at 18:00 is due by 09:00 the next morning.

**Weekends and public holidays.** These are not counted. For an issue logged after 17:30 on a Friday, the clock pauses at midnight and resumes at 00:00 on Monday. A P1 logged at 18:00 on Friday is due by 09:00 on Monday. An issue logged on a weekend or public holiday is treated as logged at 17:30 on the previous working day. A P1 logged on a Saturday is due by 08:30 on Monday.

**Enterprise.** The clock never pauses. Nights, weekends and public holidays all count.

### How to log an issue

Email [support@scrums.com](mailto:support@scrums.com) or raise a ticket. Include what failed, who is affected, when it started and the priority you believe applies.
