[Scrums.com](https://www.scrums.com) is the enterprise AI platform for software engineering. SEOP, the Enterprise AI Software Engineering Orchestration Platform, is its architecture. Mission control for engineering and product leaders.

Instantly deploy AI agents, talent, teams, tools, infrastructure, and delivery operations from one platform with live engineering intelligence, unified reporting, and predictable software delivery.

One contract. One SLA. One deploy. **Software Engineering. Sorted.™**

Operating out of offices in New York, London, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Nairobi and Lagos, we're building the future of software engineering, seamlessly orchestrated, insight-driven, and globally powered.
