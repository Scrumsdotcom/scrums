## AI backlog prioritization

**Shipped**

The backlog view now includes an AI-generated priority score for every ticket. Scores are calculated from business value signals, dependency chains, team velocity, and stakeholder-flagged urgency.

**How it works:**

- Connect your issue tracker (Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues) to sync ticket metadata
- The AI model re-scores tickets nightly and on demand
- Priority explanations are shown inline so teams understand the reasoning, not just the rank
- Scrum Masters can pin tickets to override AI ordering without losing the underlying score

The priority score is visible in the backlog list and sprint planning views. It does not automatically move tickets — all reordering requires human confirmation.

## Multi-workspace support

**Shipped**

Organizations can now manage multiple workspaces under a single billing account.

**What this enables:**

- Separate workspaces per business unit, product line, or client engagement
- Centralized billing and seat management from the account admin panel
- Cross-workspace reporting for portfolio-level visibility
- Role assignments are workspace-scoped; users can hold different roles across workspaces

To create additional workspaces, go to **Administration → Account Admin → Workspaces**.

<Note>
  Each workspace requires at least one Workspace Admin. Users can belong to multiple workspaces with independent role assignments.
</Note>

## Real-time collaboration

**In progress**

Ticket editing, sprint boards, and planning sessions will support real-time multi-user collaboration — similar to what you'd expect from a shared document editor.

**Planned capabilities:**

- Live cursor presence on sprint boards and the backlog
- Concurrent ticket editing with conflict resolution
- Collaborative sprint planning mode where multiple participants can drag, resize, and comment simultaneously
- Presence indicators showing who is viewing the same page

Real-time collaboration is in private beta. Contact your account manager to join the early access program.

## Advanced compliance reporting

**In progress**

A new compliance reporting module will provide audit-ready exports aligned with common frameworks including SOC 2, ISO 27001, and custom enterprise policies.

**Planned report types:**

- Access and permission change log
- Sprint and delivery evidence reports for audit trails
- Data residency and processing records
- Policy enforcement summaries from the governance module

Reports will be available as PDF and CSV, with scheduled delivery to a configurable email list.

## Delivery model analytics

**Shipped**

A new analytics section breaks down performance and cost data by delivery model — dedicated teams, managed delivery, staff augmentation, and on-demand.

**Available metrics:**

- Cost per story point by delivery model
- On-time delivery rate by model and team size
- Ramp time for new team members by engagement type
- Comparative velocity across concurrent delivery models

Access delivery model analytics from the main navigation under **Platform → Delivery Models → Analytics**.
