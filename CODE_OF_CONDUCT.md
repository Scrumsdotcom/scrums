# Code of conduct

This policy applies to everyone who takes part in this repository: in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

Treat contributors with respect.
Evaluate technical claims through evidence and clear reasoning.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or impersonate another contributor.
Do not use generated messages to overwhelm discussion or evade moderation decisions.

Disagreement about design, semantics, proofs, or benchmark methods is welcome.
Keep criticism specific to the work.
Correct mistakes without personal attacks.

## Enforcement

The maintainers will publish a private reporting route and a fair review process before they enforce this policy.
That process will cover confidentiality, conflicts of interest, proportionate action, and appeals.
