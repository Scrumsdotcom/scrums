# Proposed contributor conduct

This template needs owner ratification and named moderators before public collaboration begins.
It is not an active moderation policy for the parent repository.

Treat contributors with respect.
Evaluate technical claims through evidence and clear reasoning.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or impersonate another contributor.
Do not use generated messages to overwhelm discussion or evade moderation decisions.

Disagreement about semantics, proofs, or benchmark methods is welcome.
Keep criticism specific to the work.
Correct mistakes without personal attacks.

Before launch, maintainers must publish a private reporting route and a fair review process.
That process must cover confidentiality, conflicts of interest, proportionate action, and appeals.
No contact address or enforcement authority is invented in this template.
