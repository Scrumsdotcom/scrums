# /company/domains/sales-marketing

Sales & Marketing domain. Sub-modules:

- [`marketing`](./marketing), demand generation, content, campaigns
- [`sales`](./sales), pipeline, opportunity management
- [`customer`](./customer), customer success, account management, retention
- [`partnerships`](./partnerships), channel, alliance, ecosystem
- [`growth`](./growth), acquisition experiments, activation, monetization loops
- [`brand`](./brand), brand strategy, design systems, voice
- [`content`](./content), editorial, content production
- [`revenue-operations`](./revenue-operations), pipeline analytics, forecasting, commission ops

Sub-modules without manifests are organizational placeholders. The `growth` module is fully scaffolded as a worked example.
